# Hiring Pack: Senior Customer Success Manager — Mid-Market

## 1. Job Description

**Role:** Senior Customer Success Manager — Mid-Market
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Compensation:** $115,000 – $135,000 Base + 15% Variable

**About Us**
We are a 70-person B2B analytics company helping mid-market organizations turn data into decisions. We value clarity, accountability, and proactive partnership over reactive support.

**The Challenge**
You will manage a portfolio of 45 accounts representing $2.8M in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR). Our primary goal is to achieve 110% Net Revenue Retention (NRR). Currently, we face a critical challenge: late detection of renewal risks, resulting in unexpected churn. We need a strategic partner who can identify health issues early, not just at renewal time.

**Outcomes: First 90 Days**
*   **Portfolio Audit:** Conduct a deep-dive health check on all 45 accounts using Gainsight and Salesforce data. Identify the top 10 at-risk accounts and create specific save plans.
*   **Process Gap Analysis:** Map the current renewal workflow. Identify specific bottlenecks that led to recent surprise churns and propose two process improvements to shorten lead time for risk mitigation.
*   **Stakeholder Mapping:** Establish direct relationships with key decision-makers in your top 20% of accounts to ensure visibility beyond the primary contact.

**Outcomes: First Year**
*   **NRR Target:** Drive portfolio NRR to 110% through proactive upsell/cross-sell and successful saves.
*   **Risk Detection:** Reduce surprise churn to zero by implementing early-warning indicators in Gainsight.
*   **Expansion:** Identify and execute expansion opportunities in under-penetrated accounts, contributing to overall company growth.

**Responsibilities**
*   Manage the full customer lifecycle for mid-market accounts, from onboarding through renewal and expansion.
*   Develop and execute Customer Success Plans (CSPs) tailored to each account’s business goals.
*   Utilize Gainsight and Salesforce to monitor usage, sentiment, and health scores; act on insights proactively.
*   Collaborate with Sales, Product, and Support to resolve escalations and align on account strategy.
*   Drive adoption of our analytics platform to ensure customers achieve measurable ROI.

**Must-Haves**
*   Proven experience managing a mid-market B2B SaaS portfolio ($2M+ ARR).
*   Demonstrated ability to drive Net Revenue Retention (NRR) above 100%.
*   Proficiency in Gainsight (or similar CS platform) and Salesforce.
*   Strong data literacy: Ability to interpret usage metrics and correlate them with business value.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills for executive-level stakeholders.

**Nice-to-Haves**
*   Experience in data analytics or BI product categories.
*   Certification in Customer Success (e.g., CSM, CSCP).
*   Experience building or refining CS playbooks and health score models.

**Inclusive Language Check**
*   No "rockstar," "ninja," or "guru" language used.
*   Experience requirements are tied to specific outcomes (portfolio size, NRR) rather than inflated years of experience.
*   Focus on skills and behaviors rather than pedigree.

## 4. Behavioral Interview Questions

### Q1: Tell me about a time you identified a high-value account was at risk of churn before the customer did. What data or signals did you use, and what did you do?
*   **Strong Answer Contains:** Specific metrics (usage drop, login frequency, support sentiment), a clear hypothesis about the root cause, and a proactive intervention (e.g., executive business review, feature demo) that changed the trajectory. Mentions collaboration with other teams.
*   **Red Flag:** "I noticed they were unhappy because they complained" (reactive, not proactive). Or, "I just worked harder to support them" (no strategic root cause analysis).

### Q2: Describe a situation where you had to influence a Sales or Product team to prioritize an account need that wasn’t their top priority. How did you handle it?
*   **Strong Answer Contains:** Acknowledges the other team’s priorities. Uses data (revenue at risk, strategic value) to make the business case. Shows empathy and collaboration. Result is a compromise or a clear timeline.
*   **Red Flag:** "I escalated it to my manager to force them" (lacks influence skills). Or, "I told them it was urgent and they had to do it" (no collaborative approach).

### Q3: You’re managing 45 accounts. One day, three different accounts have critical issues: one is threatening to churn, one is asking for a custom feature, and one is overdue on payment. How do you prioritize and handle this?
*   **Strong Answer Contains:** Clear prioritization framework (e.g., revenue at risk, strategic value, urgency). Explains how they delegate or use tools (Gainsight) to manage the load. Shows calm under pressure. Mentions communication with stakeholders to set expectations.
*   **Red Flag:** "I’d try to do all three at once" (unrealistic). Or, "I’d ignore the payment issue since it’s not my job" (lack of ownership).

### Q4: Tell me about a time you failed to save an account or missed a renewal target. What happened, and what did you learn?
*   **Strong Answer Contains:** Owns the failure without blaming others. Analyzes the root cause (e.g., missed a signal, poor stakeholder mapping). Describes a specific process change they implemented to prevent recurrence. Shows resilience.
*   **Red Flag:** Blames the customer, product, or sales team. No clear lesson learned. Or, "I’ve never failed" (lack of self-awareness).

### Q5: How do you ensure that customers in the mid-market segment understand the value of our analytics platform, especially when they have limited data teams?
*   **Strong Answer Contains:** Focuses on business outcomes, not technical features. Mentions creating tailored demos or reports that speak to the customer’s KPIs. Uses analogies or simple language. Mentions ongoing education (webinars, check-ins).
*   **Red Flag:** "I just send them the product documentation" (no customer-centric approach). Or, "I assume they know how to use it" (lack of empathy for mid-market constraints).
