## 4. FAQ

**Q: Do I need to pay extra for Forecast?**
A: No. Forecast is included in the Pro plan at no additional cost. If you are on the Pro plan, you can turn it on immediately in Settings > Forecast.

**Q: How accurate is the prediction?**
A: Accuracy depends on data quality. In our 8-week pilot with 12 customers, average labor costs dropped by 4.2% and understaffed hours fell by 31%. The system learns from your specific sales patterns, weather, and local events.

**Q: What if my location is new?**
A: If a location has less than 8 weeks of sales history, Forecast uses a regional baseline. This is a reasonable estimate based on similar locations in your area. Once the location has 8 weeks of data, the system switches to location-specific predictions.

**Q: Can I turn Forecast off?**
A: Yes. You can turn Forecast on or off at any time in Settings > Forecast. If you turn it off, your existing schedules remain unchanged.
